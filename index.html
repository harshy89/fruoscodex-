NPC Codex

Artico's Crew

Atrissa, Overworked Steward

Female dwarf, overworked steward. A bit haggard, she helped oversee the outpost during your 2 month project

Benrick, Craftsman of Hohenheim

A Lutgehr Dwarf from Hohenheim, who never felt at home in the city. His family were killed by the blue dragon you fought

Emeric and Celestine, Esoteric Inventors

Human twins, from the United Republic, they are rather odd and love new innovations

Faisal, Ardent Poltarian

Faisal, a human from Galbard. A scholar of the Poltarian sect, deemed a heresy by the Inquisition

Gurbaz, Loyal Brawler

A half-orc warrior from the Plains Tribes. What he lacks in brains he makes up for in rage and muscle

Halcyon, Sanguine Killer

A male Drow warrior, informally aligned to The Oppressed, but functionally independent. He is a loyal fighter of Artico's

Kalora, Naive ex-Inquisitor

A female human who was placed into the Church when orphaned. She trained to be an Inquisitor but found them deplorable and defected.

Levity, Adept Diplomat

A female tiefling and representative of The Oppressed

Pages, Bashful Wizard

A male tiefling and co-leader of The Oppressed in this area of Galbard

Roliya, Genial ex-Inquisitor

A female high-elf who worked as an Inquisitor for some years. She became disillusioned and left, striking a friendship with Artico in Djenzig

Thibaut, Exiled Mage

A half-elf from Soveliss, he was evidently exiled from there and learnt magic at the New College in the United Republic

Wrenn of the Six, Aloof Spy

Wrenn of the Six, an aloof and enigmatic female tabaxi, she seems to be some sort of spy for Artico

Rogues' Union War of Succession

Anna-Maria, Fallen Aristocrat

A snobbish supporter of Jehlesse, she seeks to use her family's dwindling wealth to regain what they lost when the old empire was dissolved

Artico of Djenzig

Smarmy but wrathful tiefling wizard on a quest from revenge. Part of the same guild as Koro-Mei in the Esoterica

Jenneke Cromwell, Veteran Captain

A Captain who fought in the Great War, she is recruited to lead Lucas' forces during the succession war

Detrar and Wellyn, Embassy Officials

Detrar, a gruff duergar and Wellyn, a cowardly half-elf. Both work as attachés in the Soveliss Embassy to Djenzig

Doree and Koro-Mei, Bonded Sisters

The halfling sisters, Doree and Koro-Mei. Doree is a genial rogue and a Lucas supporter. Koro-Mei is a sorceress, more taciturn and is a member of the same Esoterica guild as Artico

Jehlesse, Ruthless Pretender

A coy and beautiful female human and natural daughter of the Union's former leader, Lord Stamper. She came close to succeeding her father.

Lucas, Warrior Bravo

A wood-elf, formerly of the Green Company of Langton. Turned rogue, he felt he was the natural successor to lead the Union after Lord Stamper

Monseigneur Johannes Wiltshire

Still insistent on using his old style from the days of the empire, this kooky aristocrat has backed Lucas with gold and more

Rémy, Son of Soveliss

Rumoured to be a base born child of House de Havilland in Soveliss, Rémy is a powerful supporter of Jehlesse

Tom Truth, Dubious Poet

A poet of questionable skill, he nonetheless commands a loyal following and is a key supporter of Jehlesse in the succession

Torrin the Black, Master Rogue

A smelly human, rogue's rogue and assassin without peer. Torrin is a Rogues' Union member through and through.
